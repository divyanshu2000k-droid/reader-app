Get your Goodreads file
Goodreads doesn't let apps connect directly any more, so you'll export a file and hand it to us. Takes about two minutes, and they email it to you.
1
Open Goodreads on the web
The app can't do this, so you'll need a browser. Sign in, then go to My Books.
2
Find Import and Export
It's in the left sidebar under Tools, near the bottom. Easy to miss.
3
Hit Export Library
A link appears on the page after a minute or two, and they also email it. Big libraries take longer.
4
Come back and pick the file
Download it to your phone, then tap below. We'll show you exactly what's in it before anything is saved.
