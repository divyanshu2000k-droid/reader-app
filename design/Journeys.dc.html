Deliberately not drawn
Conscious omissions, not misses. Each is either an Android surface, a v1.1 item, or a form Claude Code derives from the system sheets.

Twelve screens were missing and every one of them sat on a path a real user takes. The pattern worth noticing: they were all launch, recovery, confirmation and completion moments. Happy paths get designed because they are the fun part. The moments either side of them are what make an app feel finished, and they are exactly what a screen by screen audit misses until you walk the journeys instead.
